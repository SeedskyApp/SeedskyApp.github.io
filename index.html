How it works

  1. Pick a duration and a plant, from 5 minutes to 3 hours. 25 minutes makes a classic Pomodoro.
  2. Block the apps that distract you: social media, games, videos. They stay closed until the session ends. Allow the ones you need, like your course or workout app.
  3. Put your phone down. Your plant grows from seedling to full-grown tree, and joins your garden.

Questions

Is Seedsky free?

Yes. Seedsky is free on the App Store, with no ads and no account.

How does app blocking work?

Seedsky uses Apple's Screen Time for your own iPhone. Blocked apps stay closed until your session ends. Allowed apps, like your course or workout app, stay open and your plant keeps growing. Any other app asks first, and opening it starts a short grace period before your plant withers.

Can I use it as a Pomodoro or study timer?

Yes. Sessions last from 5 minutes to 3 hours: 25 minutes for a classic Pomodoro, or longer for deep work and exam revision.

What happens if I leave the app?

A grace period protects your plant: come back quickly and it recovers. Locking your screen does not count as leaving. A gentle mode pauses the session instead.

Does Seedsky collect my data?

No. Your sessions and gardens stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
